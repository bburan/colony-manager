# The breeding pair page

The pair's two parents on the left, its litters on the right.

## Litters

One row per litter, newest first: date of birth, pup count, current age,
and the wean date once it has one. The paw button records a new litter —
a litter is just a date of birth and a number of pups, not individual
animals.

Row actions:

- **Pencil** — correct the date of birth or the pup count.
- **Weaning** (the branching icon) — see below.
- **Trash** — delete the litter record. Use this only for a litter entered
  in error; weaning is what you want for a litter that is over.

Un-weaned litters are what the dashboard's **Upcoming Litters** panel
lists.

## Weaning a litter

Weaning turns a pup count into real animal records. The form takes a wean
date and one or more **destination cages**; for each cage you give a new
cage ID, a sex and a number of pups.

For every cage in the form the app creates the cage, then creates that
many animals in it — each with the litter's date of birth, the cage's
species, and this breeding pair recorded as their parentage. The litter's
wean date is then set, which takes it off the dashboard.

Weaned animals start **without IDs**. They appear on the
[cage page](/help/cage-detail), not the animal list, until IDs are
assigned.

> Splitting a litter across several cages — usually one per sex — is
> done by adding more than one cage to the weaning form, not by weaning
> twice.
