# The cage page

Details, the animals housed there, and the cage note.

## Details

Cage ID, species, sex and source. The pencil button edits them. A cage's
sex is derived from the animals in it rather than set directly.

## Animals in this cage

The same table as the [animal list](/help/animal-list), with two
differences that matter:

- **Animals without an ID are shown here.** They are not on the animal
  list. The tag button beside an empty ID assigns one — this is the normal
  way an animal graduates from "one of the pups in this cage" to a tracked
  animal.
- Two extra row actions:
  - **Move to single housing** (house icon) creates a brand-new cage,
    named as you choose, and moves just that animal into it. Used when an
    animal must be separated from its cage mates.
  - **Delete** (trash icon) appears only for an animal with no ID and no
    events — an empty placeholder created in error. Anything with real
    history cannot be deleted, only terminated.

The paw button in the card header adds another animal to this cage.

## Notes

Free text about the cage. This note is what appears in the hover popover
next to every animal in the cage on the animal list, so it is the right
place for anything that applies to all of them.
