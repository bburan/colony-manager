# The study list

Every study, with its description and the number of animals enrolled.
Click a study to open it.

A study is a named grouping and nothing more — enrolling an animal does
not change the animal, and an animal can belong to any number of studies.
Studies are the unit of comparison: the [study page](/help/study-detail)
is where you see, at a glance, which animals in a cohort have had which
procedures.

## Creating a study

The clipboard button in the header opens the new-study form: a name
(which must be unique) and a description.

## Cloning a study

The clone button on a row copies a study's name and description into a new
one, suggesting `<name> (copy)`, then `(copy 2)` and so on until one is
free. **Animals are not copied** — a clone starts empty. Use it when you
want a second cohort described the same way.

## Adding animals

Animals are added to a study from three places:

- the [animal page](/help/animal-detail)'s Studies card, one at a time;
- the [animal list](/help/animal-list)'s bulk selector, many at a time;
- the study page itself.
